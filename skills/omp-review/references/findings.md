# Findings

## Severity

Use these severities:

- `Blocker`: must fix before verification or shipping.
- `High`: likely correctness, safety, contract, or data risk.
- `Medium`: maintainability, reliability, or edge-case risk with clear cost.
- `Low`: small defect or local cleanup with bounded impact.
- `Optional`: improvement that is not required for this scope.

## Finding shape

```markdown
## Finding: <short title>

- Severity:
- Location:
- Problem:
- Cost:
- Fix:
- Verification:
```

## Finding rule

A finding is valid when it identifies an observed issue, explains why it matters, and gives a smaller or safer path forward.
